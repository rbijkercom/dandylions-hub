# LinkedIn post template

Copy this structure when drafting a post. Every factual line must trace back to a file in `sources/`.

```
Language: EN | IT | NL
Speaker: Fabio (I) | DandyLions (we)
Half: Insights (Clarity) | Consulting (Impact)
Shape: field observation | service highlight | idea explainer | event | article share | testimonial
Sources used: sources/... (list every file)

--- POST ---
[Hook: one or two lines, under ~200 characters, a concrete tension or question]

[Body: two to four short paragraphs]

[Close: one quiet line, plus an invitation such as "Begin the conversation" and a link]

--- VISUAL ---
Template: LI Post 01 / 02 / 03, LI Link 01 / 02 (see design-system/social-formats.md)
Theme: Light (ivory) | Inverted (sage)
On-image text: [the exact line, quoted from sources]
Photo: [description] or none

--- GAPS ---
[Anything missing, written as TO BE SUPPLIED BY RUBEN: ...]
```
