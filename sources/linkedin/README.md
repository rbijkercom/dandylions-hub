## Post shapes

1. **Field observation** (Insights). Open with a concrete moment or tension, name the pattern, end with one quiet line. Sources: `articles.md`, `offerings/educational-events.md`, `offerings/pattern-weaver-approach.md`.
2. **Service highlight** (Consulting). Name who it's for, the problem in their words, and how it works in steps. Sources: `offerings/*.md`.
3. **Idea explainer**. One concept per post: technical vs adaptive problems, the four competing loyalties, Ready/Set/Go, a mission is not an assignment. Source: `offerings/pattern-weaver-approach.md`, `offerings/policy-compass.md`.
4. **Event invitation**. Topic, format (keynote, masterclass, training), and the details. Event details must come from Ruben; otherwise leave them as [Date], [Time], [Venue / online], [Registration link].
5. **Article share**. The title, the dek, one line of context, and the link. Don't write or summarise an essay body; the essays aren't published yet.
6. **Testimonial**. A verbatim quote from `about.md` with its exact attribution.

## Writing rules

- The first two lines carry the post: roughly 200 characters show before "see more".
- Short paragraphs of one to three sentences. Plain line breaks, no bullet walls.
- 120–250 words is a good range. The limit is 3,000 characters.
- End with an invitation, not a hard sell. The site's call to action is "Begin the conversation"; the link is https://dandy-lions.vercel.app/en/contact (or `/it/contact`, `/nl/contact`).
- No exclamation marks. Hashtags and emoji: [TO BE SUPPLIED BY RUBEN: policy]. Until then, use none.
- Give the visual a template name from `social-formats.md` (for example "LI Post 01: Insight quote") and the line of copy that goes on it.
