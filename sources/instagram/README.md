## Post shapes

1. **Single post (1080 × 1080)**: one line that stands on its own: an archetype (the dandelion, the lion, the dandy), the Legacy line, a track-record figure, a short quote. Templates IG Post 01–03.
2. **Carousel (1080 × 1350, 4 slides)**: one idea in steps. The pack has "Technical & adaptive problems": cover, technical, adaptive, "where we come in". The same shape works for the four competing loyalties, Ready/Set/Go, or a service's four "How it works" steps.
3. **Story (1080 × 1920)**: Ready/Set/Go, or an event with a registration link sticker.

## Writing rules

- The image carries the idea; the caption adds context. Keep on-image text to a single quoted line or a few words per slide.
- Caption: the first ~125 characters show before "more". 40–150 words is a good range; the limit is 2,200 characters.
- Plain, calm sentences. No exclamation marks. Hashtags and emoji: [TO BE SUPPLIED BY RUBEN: policy]. Until then, use none.
- Links in captions aren't clickable; say "link in bio" only if Ruben confirms the bio link. [TO BE SUPPLIED BY RUBEN: bio link.]
- For each slide, give the template name, the theme and the exact on-image text.
