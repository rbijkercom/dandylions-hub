# Instagram post template

Copy this structure when drafting a post. Every factual line must trace back to a file in `sources/`.

```
Language: EN | IT | NL
Format: single post | carousel (n slides) | story
Half: Insights (Clarity) | Consulting (Impact)
Sources used: sources/... (list every file)

--- SLIDES ---
1. Template: IG Post 01 / Carousel 1/4 / Story 01 ... | Theme: Light | Inverted
   On-image text: "[exact line from sources]"
2. ...

--- CAPTION ---
[First line under ~125 characters]

[One or two short paragraphs]

[Quiet close or invitation]

--- GAPS ---
[Anything missing, written as TO BE SUPPLIED BY RUBEN: ...]
```
