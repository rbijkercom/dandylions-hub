## Our identity: the three archetypes

- **The dandelion**: Resilience as renewal. The end of a cycle, so the future can take roots. Growing through the cracks and spreading with ease.
- **The lion**: Courage, dignity, direction. Power that doesn't need to prove itself. A roar that empowers, instead of threatening.
- **The dandy**: The courage of being eccentric. Self-authorship born out of inner clarity. Acceptance of being misunderstood.

## Vision, mission, ambition, legacy

- **Vision**: A professional world where people act with awareness, teams move in alignment, and systems evolve through understanding. Where we work alongside machines — without becoming one ourselves.
- **Mission**: To help professionals, teams, and organizations grow into autonomous DandyLions — by supporting their orientation, cultivating inner reflection, and accelerating collaboration.
- **Ambition**: To be the secular custodian of organizations: caring for what emerges when people meet systems. Cultivating a human-centered approach, capable of growing alongside artificial intelligence.
- **Legacy**: Legacy isn't a mausoleum, but a root system. Impact isn't what we build—it's what continues to grow after we sow.
