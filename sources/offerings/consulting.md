## Consulting home

**What do we do about it?**

We help people and organisations navigate complexity, withstand tension and explore new horizons, with clarity and momentum.

*The way of working*: All DandyLions assignments are structured from the same attention and care for clarity, direction and momentum. We help you resolve technical tensions, and to learn how to sustain adaptive ones, so that you can grow.

- **Ready → Clarity**: Positioning yourself in the eye of the storm to watch the tempest with calm. Knowing how you are oriented and where you are headed.
- **Set → Direction**: Creating a new goal, something that was not there before. Anchoring desires in reality and asking what truly adds value, now.
- **Go → Momentum**: Aligning thought, word and action. Seizing opportunities and removing the obstacles to growth. Building a legacy that lasts over time.
