## Headline

Your project doesn't need another framework. It needs someone in your corner.

You already know the theory. What you're actually facing is a stakeholder who keeps changing their mind, a scope that has quietly doubled, and a delivery date nobody believes in anymore. Project coaching gives you a thinking partner who works on your live project — not a case study from a textbook.

## What project coaching is — and what it isn't

- **It isn't training.** You're not sitting through a curriculum. We start with whatever landed in your inbox this morning.
- **It isn't consulting.** I don't take the work off your desk or hand you my plan. In coaching, you hold the answers — my job is to help you find and act on them, so the capability stays with you after the engagement ends.
- **It is a structured, confidential space** to pressure-test decisions, rehearse difficult conversations, and get honest feedback on your stakeholder email before you send it.

## Who it's for

- New project managers who inherited the title along with the workload, and are learning on a live project
- Experienced PMs carrying something unusually complex, political, or high-stakes
- Accidental project leaders: specialists who want or need to approach an assignment as a project.
- Project managers working alone who feel the would benefit from a professional sparring partner.
- Organisations who want to build delivery capability internally rather than hire it in every time

## What we can work on

- Building a golden duo with your sponsor
- Steering without power
- Setting up decision-making
- Conveying urgency and risk so that it lands
- How to deal with the adaptive characteristics of your project
- Analyzing the primary task, anxieties and boundary disruptions within your project environment
- Protecting your own capacity and avoiding burnout

## How it works

1. **Intake**: We talk through where you are, what's stuck, and whether I'm the right fit.
2. **Goal setting**: We agree on two or three concrete outcomes for the engagement.
3. **Tailored pace**: One-to-one sessions built around your urgencies, deliverables and milestones.
4. **Review**: We check the outcomes, what you learned and what new questions arose.

## Common questions

**Do I need a certification to work with you?**
No. Plenty of my clients are running significant projects without PMP, PMI, PRINCE2, IPMA, or any formal qualification.

**Can you help with a project that's already failing?**
Yes — that's often when the call comes. We'd focus first on stabilising the situation and rebuilding trust with your stakeholders.

**How long do people usually work with you?**
Most engagements run three to six months. Some clients come back for a single session before a major milestone.
