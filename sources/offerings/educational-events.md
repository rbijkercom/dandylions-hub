## Headline

What we call relational problems are often the frictions a person carries within and acts out in a system.

Five topics, each offered in three depths. A keynote opens the question, a masterclass gives it vocabulary and frameworks, a training puts it to work in real contexts. Choose a topic and the format that fits where your team stands.

## Formats

- **Keynote** (Inspiration) · *The spark.* It inspires, gives something a name, opens a question that can no longer be unasked. It is the entry point to every topic: as a standalone moment of inspiration and reflection, or as the first step toward something deeper.
- **Masterclass** (Knowledge) · *The map.* It offers vocabulary, frameworks and the relational space to start working with the material, together, as a group. Knowledge that in the end belongs to the participants and opens new possibilities.
- **Training** (Application) · *The practice.* Skills meet real contexts. What stays with participants is a different way of standing inside their everyday challenges. It is a classic training, an experience the team goes through together, a Socratic Dialogue, a test of reality.
- **Learning-on-the-job** (Legacy) · *Long-lasting impact.* Action and reflection come together to deliver learning-on-the-job programmes, where everything weaves together: tailored goals meet processes, methods and room to learn. Today meets tomorrow.

### The weaver mind

*Topic 01 · Cognition & complexity*

For decades we’ve taught people to work like spiders: to use the thread to isolate, to capture, to build clean, precise structures. Spider silk is one of the strongest materials we know. But on its own, it isn’t enough for the complexity we live in today.

The weaver’s mind isn’t a replacement. It’s what makes the web worth spinning in the first place — weaving ideas, emotions, systems and contradictions into something that holds. Where the spider asks “did I get it right?”, the weaver asks “which threads weave into something stronger?”

This theme introduces that shift: an intellectual provocation in the keynote, a body of knowledge in the masterclass, a lived discipline in the training.

### Identity & personal boundaries

*Topic 02 · Identity & authority*

Every person carries a tension rarely named: between what they feel themselves to be, and what they show, concede, protect. Personal boundaries are the edges of one’s own individuality — the limits of what we call “I”.

When these boundaries are clear, our relationship with ourselves and with others makes sense. When they stay undefined or go ignored, people adapt, give in, shrink, or harden.

This theme explores that threshold — not to rigidify it, but to make it livable: helping people recognize needs, wants, expectations and obligations, and to say yes and no from that clarity.

The keynote names the dynamic. The masterclass maps it. The training builds the practice of living from a rooted, acknowledged sense of self.

### Functioning systems & systemic boundaries

*Topic 03 · Systems & organisation*

We’ve inherited a model built on authority: power flowing from the top, decisions made at the summit, a system managed from the outside. It’s legible, familiar — and increasingly insufficient for today’s complexity.

There’s another way to understand how systems organize: not through hierarchy, but through the dynamic interaction of their parts, where meaning and coherence emerge from relationship, not command.

The keynote opens the vision. The masterclass builds the diagnostic capacity. The training puts both to work in participants’ real systems.

### (Neuro)diversity & Inclusion

*Topic 05 · Differences and relations*

Some environments aren’t built for the minds that inhabit them. Through no one’s fault: where a workplace can’t hold complexity, people learn to shrink — to adapt, to give in, to fold themselves into shapes that were never theirs.

We start from an observation as simple as it is new: inclusion costs most to those who benefit from it least. When it doesn’t, what we’ve built isn’t inclusion but a more polite form of disappearing: assimilation.

The keynote challenges the status quo. The masterclass invites us to build environments and selves strong enough to hold what’s really there. The training is the chance to feel it on your own skin.
