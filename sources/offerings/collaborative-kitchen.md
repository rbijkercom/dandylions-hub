## Headline

Collaborative Kitchen

Organisational challenges today are complex, with no obvious owner and no simple solution.

## Quote used on the page

> The most common cause of failure in leadership is produced by treating adaptive challenges as if they were technical problems
>
> — Ronald Heifetz

We have learned that taking teams out of their meeting rooms helps them find a new relationship with the challenges they are facing. Our Collaborative Kitchen experiences strengthen self-insight and collaboration across boundaries, and create space for breakthrough thinking.

- Working with their hands, away from the usual agendas and procedures, shifts their perspective and sparks new ideas and possibilities.
- We jointly craft the recipe: a baking programme tailored to your team.

## Cards

- We offer crafted baking experiences where your team works with its hands and creates something together, tailored to the specific team's needs.
- We recognise that every team's context is different, from its professional domain and stakeholders to its mandate and organizational pressures. That's why every engagement is a tailor-made Collaborative Kitchen experience.

## In action

- During these experiences, we consciously work on how the team operates as a whole. We look at the group's dynamics and behaviour as they unfold around the workbench, and we coach the team in action.
- Baking mirrors the work itself: some steps are technical and follow the recipe, while others are adaptive and call for tasting, adjusting and trusting one another.

## Who it's for

- Project teams working on problems that cross organisational boundaries and have no single owner
- Leadership teams that need to rebuild trust before the next difficult decision
- Groups whose collaboration has become procedural rather than genuine
- Programmes where technical fixes keep being applied to adaptive challenges

## What we can work on

- Telling the difference between technical problems and adaptive challenges
- Trust and collaboration across organisational boundaries
- How the group behaves under pressure, observed as it happens
- Making space for breakthrough thinking away from the usual agendas
- Tasting, adjusting and relying on one another instead of following the recipe

## How it works

1. **Intake**: We map the team's context, its mandate and the stakeholders around it, and what it is currently stuck on.
2. **Crafting the recipe**: We jointly craft the recipe: a baking programme tailored to your team and what it needs.
3. **The kitchen**: Working with their hands, away from the usual agendas and procedures, shifts their perspective and sparks new ideas and possibilities.
4. **Reflection**: We look back at the dynamics around the workbench and translate them into the team's own work.
