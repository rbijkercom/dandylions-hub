# DandyLions brand rules

Source: Figma file ADAT7igJnBG5nvij5shy9V, "Brand identity style guide" (13 spreads) and "Full Design System", plus `docs/design-system.md` in the site repo. Exact colour and type values are in [tokens.json](tokens.json).

## Name and tagline

- The name is **DandyLions**: one word, capital D, capital L. Never "Dandy Lions", "Dandylions" or "DANDYLIONS" in running text.
- The tagline is **REFLECTION IN MOTION**. On the site it is set as "Reflection in motion". Italian: "Riflessione in movimento". Dutch: "Reflectie in beweging".
- Essence (brand guide, spread 02): "A calm botanical identity: heritage letterforms, a lion in transformation and the balance of ivory and sage. Reflection leads to clarity; clarity creates impact."
- The two halves of the brand:
  - **Insights**: *Clarity*. Speaker & trainer. Light theme (ivory). Line: "Rooted in reflection..."
  - **Consulting**: *Impact*. Project/Programme manager & consultant. Inverted theme (sage). Line: "...blossoming through action."

## Logo

The logo has three formats (brand guide, spread 03):

- **Full lockup**: lion symbol with the wordmark.
- **Symbol**: the lion mark on its own. Live file: https://dandy-lions.vercel.app/brand/logo-symbol.svg
- **Wordmark**: "Dandy" over "Lions", set in Cormorant Garamond Light.

Colour by theme:

- Light: charcoal `#2D2D2D` logo on ivory `#F5F2EC`.
- Inverted: ivory `#F5F2EC` logo on sage `#748262`.

Clear space and size (spread 04):

- Clear space: x = one wordmark cap-height, kept clear on every side.
- Starter minimum widths: 180 px, 80 px and 120 px, shown on the canvas for the full lockup, symbol and wordmark. [TO BE CONFIRMED BY RUBEN: exact mapping of each minimum width to each format.]
- Avatar use: the symbol sits inside the centre 70% of a circle crop.

## Lion motif and archetypes

- The lion motif appears as charcoal on ivory and as ivory on sage (spread 10). The split homepage uses charcoal and ivory lion artwork.
- The brand story rests on three archetypes, each with an icon (archetype dandelion, archetype lion, archetype dandy):
  - The dandelion: resilience as renewal.
  - The lion: courage, dignity, direction.
  - The dandy: the courage of being eccentric.

## Graphic devices

From the Figma social media pack and brand guide:

- **Paper grain** on ivory and on sage, very subtle (3.5% opacity).
- **Seed head**: a large dandelion seed head, cropped off an edge, at 20–60% opacity.
- **Drifting seeds**: 2–4 per layout, rising away from the seed head.
- **Gold rule**: 72–96 × 3 px, under the overline.
- **Linear vs emergent line**: shows technical vs adaptive problems (used in carousels).
- **Badges**: number and icon badges in sage muted, wine, ochre and forest.
- **Icons**: the custom line icon set from the live site (arrow up-right, check, Instagram, LinkedIn, mail, menu, eye = Vision, compass = Mission, trend up = Ambition, sprout = Legacy, lion, dandelion, archetypes, map pin, plus).

## Do

- Use only the colours in `tokens.json`. Ivory, sage and charcoal carry the layout. Gold is a quiet accent.
- Pair Cormorant Garamond (headlines, quotes, ledes) with Hanken Grotesk (body, labels, overlines).
- Use sage with ivory text only at 24 px and above (contrast 3.68:1).
- Use gold-deep `#7A663C` for overlines and accent text on ivory.
- Keep layouts calm, with generous space and few elements.
- Use copy from `sources/`. Mark anything missing in [brackets].

## Don't

- Don't add one-off colours or type sizes.
- Don't use a charcoal "dark" theme as a website theme; the site has only Light (ivory) and Inverted (sage).
- Don't use sage-deep `#646F55` or the old sage `#7F8D6C`.
- Don't use gold for text on charcoal. On charcoal, gold is for rules and seeds only.
- Don't set small ivory text on sage.
- Don't use stock icon packs (Phosphor, Lucide and so on); the brand has its own icon set.
- Don't publish a template with [bracketed] placeholders still in it.
