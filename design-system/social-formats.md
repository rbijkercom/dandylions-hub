# Social formats: LinkedIn and Instagram

Source: Figma file ADAT7igJnBG5nvij5shy9V, section "Social media pack" (October 2026). The templates are built from the brand system: Cormorant Garamond and Hanken Grotesk; ivory, sage and charcoal with gold accents; the logo component; and the graphic devices in [brand-rules.md](brand-rules.md). All template copy comes from the live site. Event details and photos are placeholders in [brackets].

## Sizes and safe zones

| Format | Size (px) | Safe zone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn post | 1200 × 1200 | 96 px margin on all sides |
| LinkedIn link image | 1200 × 627 | 72 px margin; keep the headline clear of the bottom edge (title bar) |
| LinkedIn company banner | 1128 × 191 | The page logo covers the bottom left on desktop: keep copy right of x 300 and away from the side edges (cropped on mobile) |
| LinkedIn avatar | 400 × 400 | Shown as a circle; keep the symbol inside the centre 70% |
| Instagram post | 1080 × 1080 | 88 px margin; the profile grid crops the edges, keep key content central |
| Instagram carousel | 1080 × 1350 | 88 px margin |
| Instagram story | 1080 × 1920 | Keep text between y 250 and y 1580; the top 250 px and bottom 340 px sit under the app UI |

Export at 1× PNG, or 2× for crisp text.

## Type at social scale

- Headlines and quotes: Cormorant Garamond Light/Regular, 88–128 px.
- Supporting lines: Cormorant Garamond Italic, 34–44 px.
- Body: Hanken Grotesk Regular, 22–32 px.
- Overlines: Hanken Grotesk caps with wide tracking, 18–22 px.

## Colour pairs

- Ivory + charcoal: 12.3:1, any size.
- Ivory + gold-deep: 4.9:1, overlines and accents.
- Sage + ivory: 3.68:1, large text only (24 px and up).
- Charcoal + ivory: 12.3:1; gold for rules and seeds only.

## Text limits (platform facts)

- LinkedIn post: up to 3,000 characters; roughly the first 200 characters show before "see more".
- Instagram caption: up to 2,200 characters; roughly the first 125 characters show before "more". Up to 30 hashtags are allowed. DandyLions has no hashtag policy yet: [TO BE SUPPLIED BY RUBEN].
